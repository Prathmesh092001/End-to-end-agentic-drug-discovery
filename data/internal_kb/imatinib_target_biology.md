# Target Biology: ABL1 and BCR-ABL Translocation

## Gene and Protein Details
- **Gene Symbol:** ABL1 (Abelson Murine Leukemia Viral Oncogene Homolog 1)
- **UniProt ID:** P00519
- **Kinase Family:** Non-receptor protein tyrosine kinase (TK class).

## Structural Biology
- The kinase domain toggles between active and inactive conformations controlled by an activation loop containing the DFG (Asp-Phe-Gly) motif.
- Imatinib acts as a **Type II kinase inhibitor**, locking the activation loop into the "DFG-out" inactive conformation.
- **Resistance Mechanisms:** Point mutations in the BCR-ABL kinase domain (e.g., T315I gatekeeper mutation) disrupt imatinib hydrogen bonding or prevent adopting the inactive conformation, leading to clinical resistance.

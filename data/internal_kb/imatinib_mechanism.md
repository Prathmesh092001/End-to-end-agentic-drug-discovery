# Imatinib Mechanism of Action

Imatinib (Gleevec) is a small-molecule, competitive inhibitor of specific receptor tyrosine kinases, primarily targeting BCR-ABL, c-KIT (CD117), and PDGFR (Platelet-Derived Growth Factor Receptor).

## Primary Target: BCR-ABL
- **Fusion Gene Origin:** Formed by the Philadelphia chromosome t(9;22)(q34;q11) translocation, joining the ABL kinase domain from chromosome 9 to the BCR gene on chromosome 22.
- **Biochemical Mode:** Binds to the ATP-binding pocket of the inactive conformation of the BCR-ABL kinase domain, blocking ATP binding and halting downstream oncogenic signal transduction (RAS/MAPK, PI3K/Akt, and JAK/STAT pathways).
- **Indication:** First-line treatment for Chronic Myeloid Leukemia (CML) and Philadelphia chromosome-positive Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (Ph+ ALL).

## Secondary Targets
- **c-KIT (KIT):** Inhibits kinase activity in Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumors (GIST) harboring KIT gain-of-function mutations.
- **PDGFR alpha/beta:** Inhibits signaling in myelodysplastic/myeloproliferative diseases associated with PDGFR gene re-arrangements.

# Imatinib Safety and Risk Profile

## Clinical Safety Signals
- **Hematologic Toxicity:** Common early adverse events include neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, and anemia due to bone marrow suppression; managed via dose holds or G-CSF support.
- **Fluid Retention and Edema:** Periorbital edema and lower extremity swelling occur in >50% of patients. Severe fluid retention (pleural effusion, pericardial effusion, pulmonary edema) observed in ~1-2% of patients.
- **Hepatotoxicity:** Elevation in serum transaminases (ALT/AST) and bilirubin; routine liver function monitoring required monthly.
- **Cardiotoxicity:** Infrequent risk of severe congestive heart failure and left ventricular dysfunction, primarily in patients with pre-existing cardiac risk factors.

## Drug-Drug Interactions
- **CYP3A4 Substrate:** Primary metabolism occurs via hepatic CYP3A4. Co-administration with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g., ketoconazole, clarithromycin) increases imatinib exposure, while CYP3A4 inducers (e.g., rifampin, St. John's Wort) decrease plasma concentrations.
